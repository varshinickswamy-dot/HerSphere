HerSphere uses a “career command center” aesthetic: editorial confidence paired with precise SaaS instrumentation. Dark-only, compact enough for dashboards but with generous breathing room for brand moments. Instrument Serif gives headings a human, aspirational voice while DM Sans keeps navigation and data crisp. Deep navy surfaces, violet primary actions, and restrained cyan highlights create a focused technology-startup feel. Cards use layered glass-like surfaces, subtle borders, medium-large radii and soft depth. Motion is purposeful: short fades, lift-on-hover and progress transitions. Never drift into bubblegum pink or excessive multicolor UI.
