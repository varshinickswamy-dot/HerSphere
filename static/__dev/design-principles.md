HerSphere uses a “career command center” aesthetic: editorial confidence paired with precise SaaS instrumentation. Support both themes consistently across every route, including public, authentication, student, recruiter and admin pages. Default to a bright, readable light theme for first-time visitors; preserve a user's saved light/dark preference. Light theme: warm ivory background (#FAFAF7), white surfaces, navy primary (#003B70), muted gold highlights (#B8860B), dark readable text (#17212B), and slate secondary text (#536171). Dark theme: charcoal background (#121212), raised charcoal surfaces (#1A1A1A), maroon primary (#7B2C2C), gold highlights (#FFD700), and warm off-white text (#F8F4EA). Instrument Serif gives headings a human, aspirational voice while DM Sans keeps navigation and data crisp. Cards use clear surface separation, subtle borders, medium-large radii and soft depth. Maintain accessible text contrast, visible form labels/placeholders, and readable disabled/loading/error states in both themes. Motion is purposeful: short fades, lift-on-hover and progress transitions. Never drift into bubblegum pink or excessive multicolor UI.
